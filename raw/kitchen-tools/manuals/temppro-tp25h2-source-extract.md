## Identity

- Product page name: TempPro TP25H2 Wireless Meat Thermometer.
- Manual model: Bluetooth Thermometer, Model No. TP-25H2.
- Brand note on product page: ThermoPro is now TempPro; packaging may show either brand.
- User-provided Amazon ASIN B093PNMMJX aligns with the TempPro/ThermoPro TP25H2 two-probe Bluetooth thermometer family, but Amazon title text also used "TempPro"/"Ex-ThermoPro".

## Confirmed functions and parameters

- Probes: 2 probes on the TempPro TP25H2 product page; manual text for the TP-25H2 platform describes display support for up to 4 probes.
- Probe temperature range: 14 degrees F to 572 degrees F / -10 degrees C to 300 degrees C.
- Accuracy/tolerance: +/-1.8 degrees F / +/-1.0 degrees C.
- Wireless: Bluetooth 5.0.
- Transmission range on current TempPro page: 500 ft / 140 m.
- App: ThermoPro BBQ app for pairing, monitoring, target temperatures, USDA presets, alerts, and temperature tracking.
- Display: LCD with touch backlight.
- Power: built-in/rechargeable lithium battery on current TempPro page; manual platform text references an 18650 battery.
- Unit size: 93.0 x 77.0 x 23.0 mm.
- Probe length: 205 mm; wired probe total length: 1250 mm.

## Identity

- Product: Moulinex Baguettes and Co (OW6002; sibling OW6000; retailer lists OW600230 as the full SKU).
- Retailer specs (na-kuhne, kns.ru): 1650 W, max 1500 g, 19 programs, 2 mixers, timer up to 15 h, keep-warm up to 1 h, 7 min power-backup memory. Discontinued.

## Parts (FR manual p. 2)

Bread pan (cuve), kneading paddles (mélangeurs, 2), lid with window, baking support (support de cuisson), 2 non-stick baguette plates, 2 non-stick individual-bread plates, grigne (scoring blade, very sharp), tablespoon + teaspoon (double dosing spoon), hook for removing paddles, brush (pinceau), graduated cup.
Control panel: display (weight indicator, programme, crust level, delayed start, timer), menu button (programmes), weight selector, delayed-start/time-adjust buttons (programmes 7 and 15), run light, start/stop, crust colour selector.

## Programmes (FR manual pp. 7-8)

1 Baguette; 2 Ciabatta; 3 Gressin; 4 Pain plat; 5 Pain sandwich; 6 Petit pain sucré; 7 Cuisson petits pains (10-35 min, 5-min steps); 8 Pain basique; 9 Pain Français; 10 Pain complet; 11 Pain sucré (max 1000 g dough for ready mixes); 12 Pain rapide (water max 35 C); 13 Pain sans gluten; 14 Pain sans sel; 15 Cuisson pain (bake only, 10-70 min, 10-min steps); 16 Pâtes levées (knead+rise, no bake); 17 Pasta (knead only); 18 Gâteau (1000 g only); 19 Confiture.
Programmes 1-6 = individual breads, shaped by hand, bake after shaping.

## Settings

- Default on power-up: programme 1, medium crust. Weight default 1000 g; choices 750 / 1000 / 1500 g.
- Weight not selectable: programmes 1-7, 13, 15-19. Crust not selectable: 7, 15, 16, 17, 19. Crust: clair / moyen / foncé.
- Delayed start up to 15 h; not available for programmes 1-7, 12, 13, 15-19.
- Cycle-time table (FR p. 12): extraction scrambled; per-programme times NOT transcribed. Visible: programme 1 total 2:39, dough 1:10, bake 0:47 + 0:42; programme 8 at 1000 g total ~3:24 (table order uncertain). Keep-warm 1:00 on bread programmes.

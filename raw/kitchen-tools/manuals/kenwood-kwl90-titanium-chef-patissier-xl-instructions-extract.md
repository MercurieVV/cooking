## Key Instruction Facts

- Product type in manual: **TYPE KWL90**.
- Manual sections include: manual mode, preset programmes, weighing function, creaming beater assembly, splashguard use, tool adjustment, care/cleaning, recipe, service/customer care, and troubleshooting.
- Manual "Key" identifies: high-speed outlet, slow-speed outlet, bowl-tool socket, high-speed outlet cover, mixer head, head-release lever, on/off button, display, control dial, power unit, **7L warming bowl**, **5L bowl**, **BowlBright in-bowl illumination**, low-speed outlet cover, temperature sensor, warming plate, vents, K-beater, creaming beater, whisk, dough tool, spatula, head shield, splashguard, feed-chute lid, and dough scraper.
- Weighing function: use the display weighing screen; zero/tare the scale; change units using the unit button. The scale works with the mixer head up or down, provided the appliance is on a dry, flat, stable surface and no pressure is applied to the bowl or mixer during weighing.
- Weighing capacity/precision from Kenwood sources: **1 g increments up to 6 kg**.
- Heating/warming: Kenwood FAQ says Chef Patissier XL uses **9 warming levels rather than direct degree entry** and is designed for baking tasks such as melting, proving, and sabayon; it will not boil water. Retailer specifications list the 9 levels as **24/28/35/39/44/48/54/58/64 C**.
- Timer behavior with heat: troubleshooting/FAQ says timer countdown starts only once the appliance is near the selected heat setting.
- Preset programmes from Kenwood product page: **dough proving**, **dough kneading**, **chocolate melting**, **Swiss meringue**, **egg-white whisking**, and **sponge cake**.
- BowlBright: in-bowl illumination is located under the mixer head and can be toggled on/off in settings.
- Tool adjustment: K-beater, whisk, and creaming beater are factory set but can be adjusted with a suitable spanner; **TYPE KWL90 = 19 mm**. Dough tool is factory-set and should not require adjustment.
- Splashguard note: only fit/remove the splashguard when the mixer head is locked. The manual notes splashguard use is for the 7L bowl; do not use the splashguard with the 5L bowl.
- Dough note: for bread dough, use the **7L warming bowl only**; do not fit a bowl tool when the 5L bowl is stacked in the 7L bowl.

## Capacities And Technical Parameters

- Motor: **1400 W**.
- Heating element: third-party spec pages list **300 W** heating, total **1700 W** including motor plus heat.
- Display/control: **2.4-inch TFT colour display**, Touch & Dial controls.
- Bowls: **7L EasyWarm stainless bowl** and **5L stainless bowl**.
- Capacity - dough: official Kenwood page lists **2.56 kg**.
- Capacity - egg whites: official Kenwood page lists **16**.
- Body material: metal.
- Official product weight: **12.28 kg**.

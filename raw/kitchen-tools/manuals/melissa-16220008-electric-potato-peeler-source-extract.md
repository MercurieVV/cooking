## Confirmed functions and parameters

- Functions: automatic potato peeler; salad spinner/lettuce spinner.
- 16220008 specifically includes the salad spinner basket/colander; the related 16220009 is the potato-peeler-only variant in the quick-start guide.
- Power: 85 W.
- Power supply: 220-240 V; AGDPerfekt adds 50 Hz.
- Capacity: up to 1 kg potatoes per run.
- Peeling time: approximately 2-4 minutes, depending on potato size, shape and quantity.
- Potato suitability: product pages state it handles fresh and older potatoes.
- Dimensions: product sources commonly list approximately 25.5 x 27 x 27 cm; Komputronik lists 28.3 x 28.3 x 28 cm. Treat 25.5 x 27 x 27 cm as the main repeated value and note the retailer conflict.
- Weight: 1.6 kg net; AGDPerfekt lists 1.60 kg net / 1.85 kg gross; Kaufland page lists 1.951 kg.
- Cable length: 100 cm.
- Materials: plastic/ABS body in retailer descriptions; peeling disk/grater blade.
- Safety/convenience: safety switch, transparent lid, silicone anti-slip feet, cable storage in the base.
- Runtime limit from quick-start guide: do not use for more than 4 minutes continuously.
- Peeling setup from quick-start guide: use the peeling disk in the bowl, add approximately 4 dl cold water so the outer edge of the grater is covered, place no more than 1 kg potatoes, lock lid, switch on, switch off/unplug after peeling, rinse potatoes, remove remaining eyes manually.
- Salad spinner setup from quick-start guide: remove the peeling disk, insert the salad spinner colander/basket in the bowl, add washed salad, lock lid, switch on to spin dry.
- Main parts from quick-start/user guide: salad spinner colander/basket, power cable, motor unit/base, power switch, lid, peeling disk, bowl, lid clamp.
- In-box contents from product sources: electrical base, peeling bowl, peeling blade/grater, transparent lid, salad spinner, manual/safety instructions.
